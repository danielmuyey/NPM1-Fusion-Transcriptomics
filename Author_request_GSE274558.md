1. **Biological sex** of the marrow donor(s) and of the recipient mouse.
2. **Donor pooling:** whether donor marrow was pooled, how many donors contributed to each sample, and whether any pool was shared between groups.
3. **Experimental batches:** dates or batch identifiers for transduction, transplantation, sorting, RNA extraction, library preparation and sequencing.
4. **Sampling time** after transplantation. The GEO record, Results and figure legends indicate two months, whereas the RNA-sequencing Methods indicate ten weeks.
5. **GFP-positive cell sorting:** the gating strategy (including viability and any lineage markers), post-sort purity, number of cells collected, and whether all samples were sorted on the same day with the same settings.
